# Decisions Rejected

Chronological log of designs, dependencies, protocols, and product directions that were tried, considered, or prototyped and then dropped. Required by `.cursor/rules/negative-results.mdc`.

## Format

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD Title

### Context

What was being decided and why it mattered.

### What Was Tried

The option, prototype, or path that was evaluated.

### Why Rejected

Specific reason backed by evidence (test result, benchmark, security finding, paper, RFC, audit note, or measured constraint).

### Evidence

Links to commits, branches, prototypes, sources, or `docs/research/research-log.md` entries.

### What Would Reverse This

Conditions under which this option becomes viable again.

### Paper Reuse

Whether this entry is suitable for the "Alternatives Considered" or "Limitations" section of `docs/research/paper.md`.
```
